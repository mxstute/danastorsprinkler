Straight answers

Fix the sprinklers, shrink the water bill.

A lawn going brown in Coral Springs usually isn't a lawn problem — it's a zone that quit, a head that's spraying the fence, or a pump that's losing prime. Junior finds the actual cause, tells you what it costs before he touches it, and fixes it with parts that hold.

Customers tell us the same two things afterward: the lawn came back, and the water bill went down. Leaking heads and bad coverage waste more water than most people realize.

Lawn going brown right now?

  1. Run the system manually and note which zones don't come on.
  2. Walk it. Look for heads that don't pop up, spray sideways, or shoot straight up.
  3. On a well and nothing runs at all? Don't keep cycling the pump — turn it off.
  4. Call or text (954) 592-0640 with a quick photo. That's usually enough to know what's going on.

Straight answers

Things people ask before they call.

My sprinklers won't come on at all. What is it?

On city water it's usually the timer, a failed valve, or a broken wire. On a well it's usually the pump — lost prime, bad capacitor, or a pressure switch. Either way, don't keep cycling it. Call and describe what you hear when you turn it on; Junior can often tell you what it is from that.

Do you work on well pumps?

Yes. Pump repair and replacement is a big part of the work in Coral Springs and North Lauderdale, where a lot of irrigation runs off wells. If another company told you it can't be fixed, get a second look.

Do you install new systems, or only repair?

Both. Full replacements — new piping, new heads, shutoff valve, controller — and new installs for yards that never had one. Repairs are still most of what we do.

How fast can you come out?

Call early and it's often the same day; otherwise the next. Junior works Monday through Saturday, 7 AM to 7:30 PM, and Saturdays are a normal workday, not a premium one.

Will I get a price before the work starts?

Yes, every time. Junior looks at the system, tells you what's wrong and what it costs, and you decide. If there are options — repair now versus replace, or ways to stage the work to fit a budget — he'll lay them out.

Can you actually lower my water bill?

Usually. A leaking head, a stuck valve, or a zone spraying the street wastes a surprising amount of water every cycle. Fixing coverage and setting the controller to your real watering days is where customers see the bill drop.
